About Us

Al Adl Legal - Our Story

Establishing Al Adl is an integral part of our company’s history and growth. The story of the founders of Al Adl is a remarkable one, and it is their personal story that has shaped their vision to bring rightful law and justice to Dubai. During their early days in Dubai, they faced a lot of personal difficulties and challenges, which gave them a deep comprehension of the challenges faced by those seeking legal aid. These personal experiences are what inspired them to create Al Adl. The founders understood the importance of creating a platform where the citizens of the UAE could access justice and fair treatment irrespective of their background or circumstances. They believed that everyone should be able to access quality legal services and legal representation.

With a strong determination to positively impact the lives of others, Md Azaan Salahuddin and Eldaly Ahmed Ismail Hassan embarked on their journey to establish Al Adl. Their shared vision, unwavering commitment, and relentless pursuit of justice became the foundation upon which our Legal Consultants stand today with many others joining the company and investing in its growth.

Al Adl has established itself as a reputable Legal Consultants over the years, and our team of committed attorneys carry on the spirit of the founders. We are committed to upholding the values of justice, equity, and fairness. Our goal is to provide our clients with the highest quality legal services and representation, guaranteeing that their rights and opinions are not disregarded. As a comprehensive legal firm, we offer expert counsel and representation in corporate, commercial, employment, real estate, family, criminal, and other areas. Our experienced attorneys are well-versed in the legal system of Dubai and are always up-to-date with the most recent developments. We take pride in carrying on the legacy of the founders and remain unwavering in our commitment to serving the people of UAE with distinction and compassion. As a full-service Legal Consultants, we provide expert advice and representation in areas such as corporate law, commercial law, employment law, real estate law, family law, criminal law, and more. Our team of experienced attorneys possesses in-depth knowledge of the UAE legal system and stays up to date with the latest laws and regulations.
