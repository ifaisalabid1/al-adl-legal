How To Register a Trademark Certificate?

Documents required for Trademark Registration in UAE

  • Power of Attorney (POA) – Authorizing the firm to act and file the trademark application on your behalf.
  • ID Documents – Copy of Passport / Emirates ID for Individuals and Certificate of Incorporation for Companies.
  • Trade License Copy – Required in case of company or corporate applications.
  • Clear Artwork of the Brand Name / Logo – High-resolution image in JPEG or PNG format.
  • List of Goods / Services – Details of the products or services under which the trademark will be registered.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Trademark?

A trademark is a unique name, logo, symbol, word, phrase, or design that identifies and distinguishes your goods or services from others in the market.

What Can Be Registered as a Trademark in the UAE?

You can register brand names, logos, slogans, symbols, packaging elements, and in some cases distinctive sounds or shapes, provided they are unique and not already registered.

Who Can Apply for Trademark Registration in the UAE?

Individuals, companies, local businesses, foreign entities, and entrepreneurs can apply for trademark registration in the UAE.

How Long Does Trademark Registration Take in the UAE?

The process typically takes several months, including examination, publication, and registration stages, subject to approval and no objections.

How Long is a UAE Trademark Valid?

A registered trademark in the UAE is valid for 10 years from the date of filing and can be renewed indefinitely for further 10-year periods.

What is Trademark Infringement?

Trademark infringement occurs when another party uses a registered trademark or a confusingly similar mark without permission, potentially causing customer confusion.

Can I Register My Trademark Internationally?

Yes. After registering in the UAE, you may apply for protection in other countries through international filing systems such as the Madrid Protocol.

What Happens if My Trademark Faces an Objection?

If an objection is raised, a legal response can be submitted to defend your application. Professional legal representation significantly increases the chances of approval.
